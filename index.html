About Heimdall

In the old stories, Heimdall stands at the end of the Bifrost with eyes keen enough to see a hundred leagues by night and ears sharp enough to hear grass growing on the plain. This Heimdall keeps the same watch in miniature, over servers built and run by people who actually show up.

Heimdall is a custom-built Discord bot created by Wraith, standing watch over three realms — The Merebennie, The Pantheons, and a third not yet named. It handles the parts of running a server that shouldn't need a human awake at 3am: greeting new arrivals at the gate, enforcing the rules quietly in the background, and answering questions instantly instead of making someone wait for a moderator to stir.

Nothing gets past it without notice, and nothing gets handled without care — the same charge the god at the Bifrost carried, just scaled down to the size of a Discord server.

What the Watchman Sees

Privacy Policy

Last updated September 18, 2026

Heimdall's senses are keen, but limited on purpose. Here's exactly what that means for your data.

  1. Information Heimdall collects

    Heimdall processes your Discord user ID, username, and the server and channel IDs where you use it. It reads message content only when a command is invoked (for example, the text you pass to /announce), not general conversation, and it does not read direct messages.

  2. How that information is used

    Data is used only to make Heimdall's commands work: applying moderation actions, tracking role/realm assignments, and responding to commands. It is not used for advertising or profiling.

  3. Storage and retention

    Moderation logs (warnings, mutes, kicks) and configuration data are stored for as long as Heimdall is active in a server, so staff have a record to work from. Data is deleted when it's no longer needed for that purpose or when a server removes the bot.

  4. Sharing

    Heimdall doesn't sell or share your data with advertisers or third parties. Data passes only through Discord's own systems and whatever hosting Heimdall runs on, as necessary to operate the bot.

  5. Your choices

    You can ask a server admin to have your data removed from Heimdall's records, or simply stop using its commands — nothing is collected beyond what a command invocation requires.

  6. Children's privacy

    Heimdall is only available through Discord, which requires users to be at least 13 (or the minimum age in their country). Heimdall doesn't knowingly collect data from anyone below that age.

  7. Changes to this policy

    If this policy changes in a meaningful way, the "last updated" date above will change, and significant updates will be announced across Heimdall's servers.

  8. Contact

    Questions about this policy, or requests about your data, can go to the server admins in whichever of Heimdall's servers you're in.
